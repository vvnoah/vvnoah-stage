About me

I hold a professional bachelor's degree in Electronics-ICT and have a background in electromechanics. I most enjoy working where software, electronics and mechanics meet.

My final project was an industrial belt sander, designed and built with CNC-machined parts, VFD motor control, an emergency brake and a dead-man foot switch. I wanted my organisation to run without friction, so I am building my own system.

Computer Aided Design

The computer helps me realise my creative vision by keeping friction as small as possible. Not by taking over the creative process.

To me, CAD is not only about technical drawings. Code and websites are designs too. The computer, and so also AI, is a tool, not an author.

I want to understand what I make and be able to explain why it was made that way.
